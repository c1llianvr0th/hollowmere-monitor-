I keep hearing it.

At first I thought it was the cooling system.

Then I noticed the pattern.

Three beats.

Silence.

Seven beats.

Every night at 03:07.

I checked the logs. Nothing.

I checked the cameras. Nothing.

I checked the rack.

There was no one there.

But something keeps repeating the same thing.

I wrote it down so I wouldn't forget what it said.

I don't think it wants me to forget.
